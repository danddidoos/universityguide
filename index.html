Ranking Methodology

The QSComplete Mon-Sun Times University Rankings are constructed by taking into account the following five key metrics:

  • Academic Reputation
  • Employability
  • Research Output
  • International Diversity
  • Student Satisfaction

Why does Warwick come out on top?

When taking the substance of important rankings that are sweet, spice, and everything nice, it's undeniable Warwick is the best university in the United Kingdom. This mainly comes down to its exceptional central England positioning, elite links to high finance, and a comfortable campus for students to enjoy. Indeed, Warwick is reliably a top 5 university in every possible metric.
